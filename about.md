# About Wickra Verify

Hand over a (strategy, data, claimed report) triple and get a deterministic verdict — confirmed or refuted — that anyone can recompute in ten languages.

## What it does

Wickra Verify recomputes a backtest from the strategy and data it is given, then compares the result with the report that was claimed. The answer is a verdict plus the difference, not a similarity score.

## Why it exists

Published backtest results are usually unfalsifiable — the strategy, the data and the numbers rarely travel together. When they do, checking them should be one command.
